Wat de proefopstelling laat zien

1. Er is geen uitgang. Cook mode is registered as presentation: 'fullScreenModal', which on iOS maps to UIModalPresentationFullScreen — no interactive dismissal — and the steps phase renders no close control of its own. Open it by mistake on an iPhone and the only way out is to walk through every step to the end. Try it on the left: there is nothing to press. The proposal restores an ×, 44 pt, top-right. This is the highest-severity finding in the workstream and it is not a motion question at all.

2. De timer overleeft zijn eigen stap niet. TimerDisplay is mounted as a sibling of the current step, so moving on either unmounts it (next step has no duration) or resets it via its own useEffect(..., [durationMinutes]). Start the timer on the left at step 2 and press Volgende: it is gone. Worse, it counts by setInterval decrement rather than against a deadline — press Zet weg & kom terug and the left one silently loses 90 seconds it should have spent. In a cooking app that is not a polish item; it is a wrong answer.

3. Er staan geen ingrediënten in. getMealIngredients exists on the repository and has zero screen call sites in the whole app (the friend-recipe screen renders ingredients from a fixture, not from it). Quantities are extracted at import, written to storage, and never shown to a cook again. "Bak de ui glazig" — hoeveel ui?

4. 19 pt is te klein voor een aanrecht. A phone propped against a bowl sits roughly 60–70 cm from the eye, not the ~30 cm every default type scale assumes. At 70 cm, 19 pt subtends the same visual angle as about 8 pt at reading distance. Signage practice wants ~8 mm of cap height per metre for glance recognition; at 0,7 m that is ~5,8 mm, which in Archivo is ~23 pt. Press 200 % type on both: today's layout survives (the contract holds, and that is real credit), but at 100 % the instruction is sized for a hand, not for a worktop. The number is WS-2's to set; the requirement is that cook mode's default is a glance size, not a reading size.

5. Vegen is de gebaartaal die dit publiek al kent. Every recipe in this library arrived from a feed that is operated with a thumb. Swipe is an accelerator here and never the only path: the two buttons stay, at 56 pt, because a wet finger misses and a swipe with a wet finger misses worse. Both ends resist rather than wrap — the deck says "there is nothing there" instead of teleporting you to step 1.
