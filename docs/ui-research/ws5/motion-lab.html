Remy — WS-5 motion lab

Every duration, easing and spring on this page is read from src/theme/tokens.ts. Each artboard is a fixed 393×852 element, so this is a true phone-width render at any browser width. Reduced motion sets every duration to 0ms — instant, never merely faster — exactly as resolveDuration() does. Haptic moments are marked ● and reported in the top bar; they fire under reduced motion too, because a haptic is feedback rather than motion.

The same drag bound to RATING_STEP (0,1): 90 ticks — that is what "buzzes too much" looks like, and it is exactly why the tick binds to the whole grade and not to the step.

Why this control above all others
It is the only place in Remy where a finger sets a continuous value. A slider with no tactile detent is the commonest single source of "this app feels cheap", and it is the cheapest thing on this page to fix: roughly six lines inside the PanResponder that already exists.

4 · Het blad — een handvat dat liegt, en een dat dat niet doet

Left: today. A drag handle is drawn on three of the four sheets and none of them drags; the sheet also has no exit at all — visible flips false and it is simply gone. Right: motion.springDefault (damping 20, mass 1, stiffness 180) integrated for real, not approximated with a bezier. Drag it by the handle or the body. Past 25 % of its height, or on a fast flick, it dismisses; short of that it springs home. That token is defined in tokens.ts and is consumed by nothing.

The arithmetic of springDefault, so nobody has to take it on faith: ζ = 20 / (2√180) = 0,745; ωn = 13,4 rad/s; overshoot 3,0 %; 2 % settling in ~400 ms. It is a good, slightly lively sheet spring — closer to iOS than to Material — and whoever wrote it into tokens.ts chose well. It has simply never been used.

5 · De wachtwoorden — checkpoints, skeletten, de puls

Left: the import narration. Today each circle flips as a hard boolean with no transition, and the third never fills on a timer (correct — and the one thing about this screen that is already right). The proposal fills the circle over durationNormal, draws the stem down to the next row, and lets the in-flight row breathe on a 2,4 s opacity cycle — calm waiting, not a spinner, and dead still under reduced motion. Right: skeletons with no shimmer, and the timer pulse: opacity only, three times, never scale.
