The one honest caveat. accent and positive used to be different colours (blue and green); they are now two greens, kept apart by lightness and chroma rather than by hue. Measured distance: 0.138 in light and 0.165 in dark, against 0.207 and 0.183 for the pair they replace. The light scheme genuinely gives up about a third of that separation in order to be white with green accents. If the timer circle above does not read as changed, that is the trade showing, and the fix belongs in the component (a different glyph or shape), not in a brighter green.
